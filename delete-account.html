Account and Data Deletion Request

At T.C., we value your privacy. If you no longer wish to use Know Ball and want your account and all associated personal data permanently deleted, we provide simple ways to do so. Initiating an account deletion will permanently erase your profile and all associated history from our systems.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

When your account is deleted, we permanently erase the following from our active databases:

  • Your account profile (email address, username, authentication tokens).
  • Your entire gameplay history, high scores, and leaderboard entries.
  • Any telemetry, analytics, or coin transaction logs linked directly to your user ID.

Note: If you have purchased an auto-renewing subscription, please ensure you cancel it directly through your Apple App Store or Google Play account. Deleting your account with us does not automatically cancel third-party platform billing.

How to Delete Your Account and Data

Method 1: Immediate In-App Deletion (Recommended)

The fastest way to delete your account and instantly wipe all data is directly inside the app:

  1. Open Know Ball on your device.
  2. Navigate to the Settings screen.
  3. Go to the Account & Security section.
  4. Tap the Delete Account button and confirm your choice.

Result: Your account will be closed immediately, and all associated personal data will be triggered for instant, permanent erasure from our active databases.

Method 2: Web / Email Request

If you have already uninstalled the app, or if you have a special request regarding additional data checks or specific telemetry deletion, you can submit a request via email:

  1. Send an email to support@knowball.world.
  2. You must send this email from the exact email address associated with your Know Ball account to verify your identity.
  3. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request - Know Ball
  4. State clearly whether you want a standard account deletion or have a special request regarding your personal data.

Processing Time: Email and manual web requests are verified and processed within 30 days.

For more detailed information on how we handle and protect your information, please read our Privacy Policy.
